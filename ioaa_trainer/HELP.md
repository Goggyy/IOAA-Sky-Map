# Sky Map Trainer

Practise reading the sky with randomly generated star maps.

## Exercises

- **Celestial Pole and Related:** Mark the equator, directions, pole, and latitude.
- **Ecliptic:** Locate the ecliptic and visible zodiac constellations.
- **Find Constellation:** Locate a named constellation.
- **Name Constellation:** Identify one circled on the map.
- **Name Star:** Identify a circled star and its Bayer designation.
- **Name Star from Alt/Az:** Identify a star from its coordinates.
- **Estimate LST:** Use the marked meridian and star patterns.
- **Missing Stars:** Identify 1–7 stars removed from the map.

Constellation sets: **All, Zodiac, Northern, Southern, Winter Sky, Summer Sky**. Star sets: **Top 60** or **Extended**. Note that fixing sky generation may not show enough targets from every set.

**Reveal** shows the answer and stops the timer; **Hide** hides it without restarting the timer. **New** starts another question. **Analyse** opens the same sky with optional reference layers.

## Tools

- **Sky map generator:** Enter observer coordinates or pick them on the expandable world map. Enter UTC, local time, LST, or MJD; local time follows the selected time zone and daylight saving. Alternatively, choose the zenith directly. Adjust map layers, orientation, coverage, and star display; save the chart as PNG.

- **Coordinate Converter:** Convert between RA/Dec and Alt/Az using latitude and LST or hour angle. The celestial-sphere diagram shows the P–Z–★ triangle.

## Settings

Choose random or fixed location and time, Solar System objects, colours, sky coverage, magnitude limit, star size, and map rotation. Saved settings apply to new exercises and initialise the analysis and generator views.
